### Fixed

- **Voice typing no longer cuts off a recording in progress.** If the recorder is already recording — a meeting you started with its button, for example — pressing the voice-typing shortcut now does nothing to the recorder and tells you to stop the recording first. Before, ending the dictation also ended that recording.
- OpenBean only ever ends a recording that it started itself.

### Changed

- This window now shows what is new in an update.
- The "put back into the case" sync trigger is confirmed on hardware: put the recorder back and new recordings download on their own.
- The log now says why an automatic sync did not run.
