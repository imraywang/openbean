### New

- **Heart mode.** Wear the recorder all day and put it back in its case: OpenBean syncs the day, transcribes it, splits it into conversations and writes that day's diary and to-dos. A strip on the Heart mode page shows the four steps — download, transcribe, write diary, clean up the recorder — and you get one notification when the day is ready. Heart mode is off until you finish a short introduction, where you choose whether synced recordings are deleted from the recorder (on by default) and how long audio stays on this Mac (7 days by default; transcripts are always kept). The diary uses the LLM service you set up; with a cloud service, transcript text is sent to it.
- **Voice typing while the recorder is recording.** OpenBean listens in on the running recording instead of refusing; the recording is not paused, stopped or deleted. What you dictate is left out of the day's diary.
- **Highlight marks.** Double-tap the recorder during a recording; the marks show up in the transcript and in the diary.
- **Bluetooth downloads resume.** An interrupted sync continues where it stopped.

### Changed

- **A new main window.** Heart mode, Recordings, Voice typing and Recorder each have their own page with their own settings; shared settings are at the bottom of the sidebar. The separate Settings window is gone.
- **The menu-bar icon opens the main window.** Right-click it for the menu. *Check for Updates* is now in Settings, and so is *Quit OpenBean*.
- Jumping around in a recording's audio is now accurate.
- OpenBean reconnects to the recorder in about 2 seconds after launch instead of about 17.
- Heart mode transcripts are hidden from agents (MCP) unless you allow them.
